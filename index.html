Our Plan for the Abuja Municipal/Bwari

Our vision is backed by a concrete, actionable plan. We will focus on sponsoring key legislation, launching transformative projects, and ensuring every resident feels the impact of good governance.

My Legislative Agenda

  • Abuja Municipal/Bwari Youth Employment & Empowerment Bill: To create a legal framework for the Ward Job Creation and Community Security programs.
  • Abuja Municipal/Bwari Original Inhabitants' Rights & Compensation Bill: To address historical grievances and ensure fair compensation and representation.
  • SME Development & Funding Bill: To establish a dedicated fund and support system for small businesses in the Abuja Municipal/Bwari.
  • Abuja Municipal/Bwari Satellite Towns Infrastructure Renewal Bill: Mandating federal budget allocation for the urgent upgrade of roads, water, and power in satellite towns.
  • Abuja Municipal/Bwari Education Excellence Bill: To secure funding for the Generational Scholarship Fund and upgrade public school facilities.

Our Key Projects

  • Satellite Town Road Network Overhaul: A comprehensive project to rebuild and pave major access roads in Lugbe.
  • Abuja Municipal/Bwari Independent Power Project: Initiating a solar power grid for satellite towns to guarantee a minimum of 18 hours of electricity daily.
  • Kuje Agricultural Hub & Market: Establishing a modern hub for farmers to access storage, processing facilities, and a direct-to-consumer market.
  • Gwagwalada Tech & Vocational College: Building a state-of-the-art institution focused on digital skills, craftsmanship, and entrepreneurship.
